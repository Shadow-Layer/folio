# Questions Before Capital

What exactly is being purchased?

Who owns the underlying asset?

What is the payment structure?

What fees apply?

What assumptions support the projected outcome?

What happens if construction is delayed?

What happens if the expected valuation is not achieved?

Which figures are verified?

Which figures are projections?

What happens if the investor needs to exit?

What risks are not obvious from the brochure?

Information presented here is for research and educational purposes. Opportunities and figures should be independently verified before any financial decision.
