# Opportunities

## What are people building?

Capital starts with attention. These are research subjects to examine, not automatic investment recommendations.

- **Property** — places, uses, ownership, construction, and the assumptions around value.
- **Business** — products, operations, customers, margins, and the work required to make an idea durable.
- **Development** — how a plan moves from land and approvals to a built environment.
- **Research** — the evidence, missing information, and questions that shape a view.

### Why it is interesting

Property brings together location, use, ownership, construction, financing, operations, and time. It is a useful place to learn how a beautiful possibility becomes an understandable proposition.

### What we know

This entry is a framework for investigation. It contains no asking price, projected return, funding target, participant count, completion date, or claim of availability.

### What we don't know

The underlying asset, title, approvals, costs, operator, financing, timeline, market evidence, and exit conditions would all require independent verification before any financial decision.

### Questions to investigate

- What exactly is being purchased or developed?
- Which documents establish ownership and permission to build or operate?
- Which costs are known, and which are assumptions?
- What would make the underlying use valuable over time?

This is an educational research subject, not a solicitation or recommendation.
