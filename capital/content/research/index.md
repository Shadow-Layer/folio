# Research

## A way of looking

Every opportunity should move through four distinct layers:

- **Fact** — an observable statement supported by a source.
- **Source claim** — what a brochure, operator, seller, or other material says.
- **Interpretation** — a reading of the material, with its limits made visible.
- **Assumption** — an input that still needs testing before it can support a decision.

The fifth layer is the **open question**: what we still need to know.

## The research note

The Capital surface is a notebook for learning how capital moves through property and business. It is intentionally slower than a sales page. A claim should be inspectable, an assumption should be named, and uncertainty should remain visible.

No current opportunity facts are published here without source material. When a case study is added, the source, date, scope, and unresolved questions will travel with it.

## Beautiful, then understandable

Beautiful.

But that's not the question.

The question is whether the opportunity makes sense — what is known, what is assumed, what can change, and who carries each risk.
