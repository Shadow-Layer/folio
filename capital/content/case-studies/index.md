# Case Studies

## Source threshold

No case study is published yet. Legitimate Azizi Venice source material was not present in the supplied repository, so no claims about the project, location, masterplan, residences, infrastructure, numbers, or status are presented here.

When source material is available, a case study will separate:

- **Source claim** — what the material says.
- **Verified fact** — what can be independently established.
- **Interpretation** — what the evidence may suggest.
- **Open question** — what still needs verification.

The absence of a published case study is a deliberate evidence boundary, not a missing sales page.
