## Opportunity details

### Description

The catalogue title is “Digital Designer”; the individual listing's role heading says “Digital Artist.” The work focuses on assessing images and identifying visual differences and artifacts.

### Responsibilities

- Upload original digital images or photographs according to project specifications.
- Compare original artwork with AI-generated counterparts and document visible differences.
- Identify issues involving anatomy, color, lighting, perspective, texture, detail, and common generated-image artifacts.
- Provide concise, specific written feedback and collaborate with project coordinators and contributors.

### Required qualifications and skills

The listing emphasizes visual analysis, attention to detail, and clear communication. It states no prior AI experience is required.

### Preferred qualifications

- Familiarity with image editing or creation tools.
- Experience identifying generated-image artifacts; image evaluation or quality assurance experience is advantageous.
