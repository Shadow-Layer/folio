## Opportunity details

### Description

The project aims to improve how AI systems respond to federal student-loan questions, prioritizing accurate and useful guidance.

### Responsibilities

- Review sample questions for whether they reflect real borrower inquiries.
- Draft and refine rubrics covering repayment, forgiveness, relief, servicing, deferment, forbearance, and default scenarios.
- Evaluate AI-generated answers for inaccuracies, missing details, and outdated guidance.
- Provide written feedback and identify incorrect or ambiguous guidance.
- Collaborate with other experts to keep rubrics and feedback aligned with current practices.

### Required qualifications and skills

- At least one year of direct experience advising federal student-loan borrowers in a relevant context.
- Knowledge of repayment strategies, income-driven repayment, forgiveness programs such as PSLF, deferment, forbearance, and default resolution.
- Practical knowledge of loan servicing and borrower communication.
- Familiarity with current federal student-loan regulations and practices.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.

### Preferred qualifications

Experience reviewing or developing guidance documents, rubrics, or training materials is relevant.
